# Budgets, recurring payments and alerts
<!-- endpoints: budgets_page#budgets, recurring_page#recurring-payments-and-subscriptions, alerts_page#alerts, month_close_page#month-close -->
<!-- icon: bi-pie-chart -->

Set monthly limits for your spending, list the payments that repeat, and let the Hub warn you about what needs attention. At the start of each month, a short checklist helps you close the month before.

## Recurring payments and subscriptions

Open **Recurring & subscriptions** under Money in the sidebar. The tiles show:
- **Fixed costs a month**, with the yearly figure;
- **Recurring income**;
- **By type (monthly)**.

### Suggestions found in your transactions

The Hub looks for payments to the same payee at a regular gap: weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly. The amounts must be similar, or the payee must look like a subscription, bill or insurance premium (for example Netflix, Airtel, LIC). These appear under **Found in your transactions**, with **Why**, **How often**, the amount, the last and next dates, and the account.

To accept suggestions:
1. Tick the ones that really repeat.
2. Click **Add ticked**.

The cross on a row ("Not recurring — don't suggest again") hides that payee for good.

### Adding one yourself

1. Under **Your recurring payments**, click **Add**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Amount (₹)**, **How often**, **Next payment** and **Type**. Types are Subscription, Bill / utility, Insurance, SIP / investment, Rent, Income and Other.
3. Choose **Paid from**, the account that pays it.
4. Click **Save**.

The pencil edits a payment. The stop button ("Stopped / cancelled") ends it. Ended and ignored payments are listed at the bottom, under "Stopped and ignored". There, **Restore** brings one back, and **Suggest again** lets an ignored payee be suggested again.

Recurring payments, apart from income, appear on the dues calendar (see [Accounts and net worth](help:accounts-and-net-worth)). A payment paid by credit card is part of the card bill, so it is not counted again in the cash you need.

## Budgets

Open **Budgets** under Money. A budget is a monthly limit for one expense Head, for example Food & Dining. The same limit applies every month.

### Setting budgets

1. Click **Suggest from last 3 months**. Each Head without a budget gets its three-month average, rounded up to the next ₹500, if it is ₹500 or more.
2. Change any figure in the **Monthly budget (₹)** column. The grey hint in an empty box is the monthly average.
3. To budget a Head with no spending yet, open **Set a budget for another head**.
4. Click **Save budgets**.

To remove a budget, clear its box and save.

### Reading the page

The month buttons at the top show the last six months. The tiles show:
- **Budgeted**: the total of all budgets and how much of it you have spent;
- **Left to spend**: in the current month, about how much you can spend per day;
- **Spent without a budget**: spending in Heads that have no limit.

For each Head, the table shows a progress bar, **Spent**, **Monthly avg** and, in the current month, **On pace for**. A Head turns amber when it passes 85% of its budget or is on pace to go over. It turns red when it is over. Click a Head's name to see its transactions.

> **Good to know:** Spending here is net of refunds, and leaves out transfers and expenses you will claim back. It follows the top-bar Viewing filter, so you can budget for one book.

## Alerts

The bell in the top bar shows how many alerts you have. It turns red when one is urgent. Click it, or open **Alerts & month close** under Home.

You may see alerts like these:

| Alert | What to do |
|---|---|
| Card bill overdue, or due within 5 days | Pay it; the alert links to the card |
| Card bill past the due date, payment not seen yet | Add the next card or bank statement |
| Bank balance short of the next 30 days' dues | Move money in; see the dues calendar |
| Cards above 30% utilisation | Pay down early; see Card & loan tools |
| Accounts that need a newer statement (over 40 days old) | Add statements |
| Accounts without a balance | Add a statement or type the balance |
| Transactions to categorise | Open the "To review" list |
| A Head over or close to its budget | Check Budgets |
| A bullet loan falling due within 60 days, or already past due | Repay or update the loan |
| A deposit maturing within 30 days | Decide to renew or move the money |
| Interest or late-fee charges in the last 75 days | Find out why they were charged |
| Possible recurring payments found | Review them on Recurring |
| Open buy / sell instructions | See Investments |
| Close last month (from the 5th of the month) | Open the checklist |

Each alert has a button that takes you to the right page.

To hide an alert for a while, click **Snooze** and choose **Until tomorrow**, **For a week** or **For a month**. Snoozed alerts are listed under **Snoozed**, where **Show again** brings one back.

## Month close

The month close is a short checklist that makes sure last month is complete. The **Month close** card on the Alerts page shows how far you are. **Open checklist** opens it.

Five steps are checked automatically:
- **Statements added for every account**;
- **Every transaction categorised**;
- **Card bills paid on time**;
- **Recurring payments reviewed**;
- **Net worth recorded at month end**.

Four steps you tick yourself:
- **Cash in hand and wallets counted / updated**;
- **Contract notes for the month added (Investments)**;
- **Budgets reviewed for next month**;
- **Bills, receipts and tax documents filed**.

A step that is not done has a **Fix** link to the right page.

To close the month:
1. Work through the steps.
2. Click **Close** followed by the month name. If some steps are still open, the Hub asks you to confirm.

Closing records today's net worth. A closed month shows a tick in the month buttons at the top. **Re-open month** undoes it.

The right side shows the month in numbers (**Income**, **Spent**, **Invested**, **Saved**, **Transactions**), the top Heads and your budgets.

## Good to know

- Alerts are worked out fresh each time, so a fixed problem disappears on its own.
- The Hub can also send payment reminders and a weekly report by e-mail. See [Reminders by mail](help:reminders-by-mail).
