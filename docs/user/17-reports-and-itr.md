# Reports and ITR

<!-- icon: bi-file-earmark-spreadsheet -->

Download your figures as Excel, PDF or CSV for your records or your chartered accountant, and see an ITR-relevant summary for each financial year. The **Report** button in the top bar exports whatever you are looking at.

## The Reports & ITR page

Open **Reports & ITR** under Money in the sidebar. The buttons at the top choose the financial year, for example **FY 25-26**. Every year that has transactions or trades is listed. The dates of the chosen year are shown beside them.

The page has four parts.

### Income & expenses by category

- **Breakdown Excel** downloads the Breakdown for the year. Its sheets are the Type › Head › Sub-head › Item totals, monthly expenses, monthly income, totals by entity, person and account, and every transaction.
- **Open Breakdown** opens the same figures on screen (see [Transactions and categories](help:transactions-and-categories)).

Transfers, such as card bills, moves between your own accounts and loan principal, are kept out.

### Cash-flow report

**Cash-flow Excel** gives money in and out for every transaction of the year, including transfers. Use it to reconcile with your bank and card statements. It has four sheets: **Monthly Summary**, **Category Analysis**, **Account Summary** and **All Transactions**.

### Transactions (CSV)

**CSV** downloads every transaction of the year. Each row has its name, original description, Type, Head, Sub-head, Item, entity, person, account, debit, credit and notes. It opens in Excel or any spreadsheet.

### ITR-relevant summary

This summary collects, for the chosen year, the figures you need when you file your income-tax return.

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Income received | Salary / consultancy fees, pension, money from your own business, savings and FD interest, dividends, receipts from people, other income still "to review" |
| Deduction-related payments | Life insurance (80C), health insurance (80D), PMSBY / PMJJBY, tuition fees (80C), home loan interest (24b), home loan principal (80C), donations (80G) |
| Taxes paid | Advance and self-assessment tax |
| Capital gains on listed shares | Sale value, short-term gains (held 12 months or less) and long-term gains (held more than 12 months) |

How the figures are worked out:
- Only your personal money counts. Transactions in a company book, and expenses you will claim back from a client, are left out.
- Home loan principal is the EMIs paid to the home loan account, less the interest.
- Capital gains match each sale with your earliest purchase first (first in, first out). Brokerage and charges are added to the cost of buys and taken off the sale value.
- **Show … matched sale(s)** lists every sale with the date bought, the term and the gain.

If you sold shares whose purchase is not in the Hub, the summary says so, for example "Sold without a buy in the Hub: 50 shares". Add the opening holding on [Trades](/investments/trades) to complete the figure.

If transactions in the year are still "To review", a note links to them. Categorise them first, so that the figures are complete.

> **Important:** The summary is for reference only. It comes from the statements and contract notes in the Hub. Always check it against Form 16, Form 26AS / AIS and your broker's capital-gains statement before you file.

## The Report menu in the top bar

Every page has a **Report** button in the top bar. It exports the view you are looking at.

The menu's heading shows what will be exported: "All books & accounts", or the book, account or broker chosen in the Viewing filter. It also shows the period, if the page has one. Pick **Excel** or **PDF**.

To export one account's statement:
1. In the top-bar Viewing filter, choose the account, for example "SBI Card •••• 1234".
2. On [Transactions](/transactions), choose the dates with **From** and **To** and click **Apply**.
3. Click **Report**, then **Excel** or **PDF**.

On most pages the report contains:
- **Summary**: the view, the period, number of transactions, income, expenses, net investment, savings, and transfers;
- **By head**: income and expenses for each Head;
- **By month**: money in, money out and net;
- **By account**: money in, money out and number of transactions;
- **Transactions**: every transaction with category, book, person and notes;
- **Linked transfers**: confirmed transfers in the period.

The period is the **From** and **To** of the page you are on. If the page has none, it is the current financial year. When the Viewing filter is set to a broker, the report is that broker's trades and holdings instead.

PDF reports are landscape A4, with page numbers and the date they were made. Excel reports have one sheet per section.

### Pages with their own report

Some pages replace the general report with their own:

| Page | Report |
|---|---|
| Transfers & links | Flows and every confirmed link |
| Director’s account | Opening and closing balance and every movement |
| Reimbursements | Claims, expenses and pay-backs for the payer |
| Loans with people | Every loan and every movement |
| Holdings by broker | Trades and holdings by broker |
| Demat check (CDSL / NSDL) | The demat reconciliation |

The **Trades** page has its own **Report** button next to its filters. It exports the trades you filtered.

## Good to know

- Reports contain your financial details. Store downloaded files safely, and delete copies you no longer need. See [Security and privacy](help:security-and-privacy).
- The **Breakdown Excel** and **CSV** follow the top-bar Viewing filter. The cash-flow report always covers all your accounts, and the ITR summary always covers your personal money only.
- For a different period than a whole year, use **Export to Excel** on the Breakdown page, or **CSV** on Transactions, after choosing the dates.
