# Choosing what you use

Turn on only the parts of the Hub you need — savings, cards, loans, business books, investments, demat and more — and add any account that is missing, whenever you like.

## The first time you log in

Right after your first log-in the Hub asks **What do you use?** Tick what applies to you and click **Continue**. The menu, the search (Ctrl+K) and the tabs on **Accounts** then show only those parts.

You can change your choice at any time: **Settings › Features & accounts**.

> **Good to know:** Unticking a feature never deletes anything. Its pages are only hidden; tick it again and everything is back exactly as it was. Statements, transactions, categories, reports and settings are always on.

## The features

| Group | Feature | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday money | Savings & current accounts | Bank statements, balances, UPI / NEFT transfers |
| Everyday money | Credit cards | Card statements, bills, due dates, reward points |
| Everyday money | Loans & EMIs | Home, personal and gold loans, EMIs and their schedules |
| Everyday money | Deposits & schemes | FDs, RDs, PPF, EPF, NPS, bonds |
| Everyday money | Wallets, cash & other assets | Wallets, cash in hand, gold, property |
| Everyday money | Budgets & regular payments | Monthly budgets, subscriptions, recurring payments |
| Everyday money | Failed payments & refunds | Payments that failed and came back, refunds you are waiting for |
| Everyday money | Loans with people | Money lent to or borrowed from friends and family |
| Business | My own business (company books) | Separate books for your company, the director's account |
| Business | Expenses a client pays back | Costs you pay for a client and claim back |
| Investing | Stocks, ETFs & brokers | Contract notes, portfolio and plan, trades, holdings by broker |
| Investing | Demat check (CDSL / NSDL) | Comparing CDSL / NSDL statements with each broker |
| Investing | Wealth planner & tools | The compounding planner, card and loan calculators |
| Automation | Mail inbox | Statements arriving by mail are read and imported |
| Automation | Reminders & reports by mail | A mail the day before a payment is due, and a weekly dues report |

A few features depend on each other:

- **Demat check** needs **Stocks, ETFs & brokers** — untick investments and the demat check is turned off too.
- **Card & loan tools** stay available while either **Credit cards** or **Loans & EMIs** is on, even if the wealth planner is off.

If you open the address of a page whose feature is off (for example from a bookmark), the Hub brings you to this page so you can turn it on.

## Adding a missing account

The lower part of the page, **Your accounts — add anything that is missing**, lists each kind of account you use with how many you have. Click **Add** next to a kind to open the usual add-account form for it (see [Accounts and net worth](help:accounts-and-net-worth)).

- Bank accounts and credit cards are also created for you when you add their statements on [Add Statements](/import).
- **Demat accounts (CDSL / NSDL)** opens the form on the [Demat check](help:demat-check) page.
- **Company books** takes you to **People & entities**, where you add your company as a book (see [Books, people and entities](help:books-people-and-entities)).

## Questions

**I turned something off by mistake — is my data gone?** No. Tick it again on this page; all its data is still there.

**Do turned-off accounts still count in my net worth?** Yes. Hiding a kind of account only removes its tab from the menu and the Accounts page; your totals still include every account you have.

**Does each user choose separately?** Yes. Every user has their own choice, stored with their own encrypted data.
