A tour of the Hub's screen: the Home page, the sidebar, the top bar with its Viewing filter, search, reports and help, and the light and dark themes.

## Home

**Home** is the first page you see after logging in. It answers "where do I stand today?" at a glance.

- **The greeting** uses your name. If the Hub doesn't know it yet, click **Tell the Hub your name**.
- **Quick buttons**: **Add Statements**, **Add account** and **Insights**.
- **Four summary cards**: **Net worth**, **Cash in banks**, **Due in 30 days** and **Loans & EMIs**. Click a card to open the page behind it.
- **Next 30 days**: card bills, EMIs and other payments coming up. **Calendar** opens the full **Dues calendar**.
- **Needs attention**: the most important alerts, such as a card bill due soon, or bank balances that are short of the next 30 days' dues.
- **Close** the month: a short monthly checklist of statements, categories, bills and net worth.
- **Cash flow**: income, spending, investments and savings for the month, with a chart.
- **Spending by head**: this month's spending per head, against your budget if you have set one.
- **Net worth** chart: recorded each day you open the Hub.
- **Card utilisation**: how much of each card's limit is used, and a link to **Best card to use today**.

> **Tip:** The page fills up as you add statements. Start with [Adding statements](help:adding-statements).

## Insights

**Insights** (in the **Home** group of the sidebar) shows charts of where your money goes: monthly income against expenses, expenses by head and by account, recent transactions and your top payees. **View All** opens the full **Transactions** list.

## The sidebar

The sidebar on the left holds every page. At the top are two shortcuts:

- **Add Statements**: the page where you bring in bank and card statements.
- **Mail inbox**: statements arriving by mail. A number shows how many are waiting for you. See [Mail inbox](help:mail-inbox).

Below them the pages are grouped. Click a group name to open or close it. The Hub remembers which groups you keep open.

| Group | Pages |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Overview, Insights, Dues calendar, Alerts & month close |
| **Accounts** | All accounts, Bank accounts, Credit cards, Loans, EMIs, Deposits & schemes, Brokers & demat, Wallets & assets, Net worth |
| **Money** | Transactions, Breakdown, Recurring & subscriptions, Budgets, Transfers & links, Loans with people, Failed & refunds, Reimbursements, Director's account, Reports & ITR |
| **Investments** | Portfolio & plan, Trades, Holdings by broker, Demat check (CDSL / NSDL), Asset types, Import broker files |
| **Plan** | Wealth planner, Card & loan tools |
| **Settings** | Categories, Payee rules, Features & accounts, People & entities, Statement passwords, Mail inbox, Reminders & reports by mail, Backup & restore, Help & user manual, Report a problem, Users & security |

The bottom of the sidebar shows the current financial year, your name and the version numbers of Finance Hub and its Statement Reader.

> **Good to know:** On a narrow window or a small screen the sidebar hides. Click the menu button at the top left to show it.

## The top bar

Every page has the same bar at the top. From left to right: the page title, the Viewing filter, then **Search**, the alerts bell, the theme button, **Report**, the help button, the lock button and the power button.

### The Viewing filter

Next to the page title is a funnel button. It shows **All** when you see everything. Use it to look at only one part of your money. Every page then follows the same choice.

Click the funnel and choose one of these:

- **Everything**: all books, accounts and brokers.
- **Books**: only one book, for example **Personal** or your company "ABC Traders Pvt Ltd". This list appears once you have more than one book. See [Books, people and entities](help:books-people-and-entities).
- **One account**: a single bank account, credit card, loan, deposit, wallet or asset, listed under its kind.
- **Brokers**: money moved to and from one broker. The **Report** menu then gives that broker's trades.

While a filter is on, the funnel is highlighted and a band at the top of the page says **Showing only** followed by your choice. Click **Show everything** in that band to remove the filter.

> **Good to know:** The filter is forgotten when you lock the Hub or log in again. Reminder mails always cover everything, whatever the filter shows.

### Search (Ctrl+K)

Press **Ctrl+K**, or click **Search**, to search the whole Hub. You can also press the **/** key when you are not typing in a box.

Type a few letters. The Hub finds:

- pages, by name;
- accounts, by name, bank or the last digits of the number;
- loans and EMIs;
- transactions, by description, payee, notes or amount;
- categories, stocks, recurring payments and people.

Use the up and down arrow keys to move, **Enter** to open and **Esc** to close.

> **Tip:** To find a payment, type part of the amount, for example `2499`, or a word from the narration, such as a shop name or "UPI".

### The Report menu

**Report** downloads a report of what you are looking at, as **Excel** or **PDF**. The top line of the menu tells you what the report will cover.

- Normally it covers the current Viewing filter for the current financial year: a summary, money in and out by head and by month, the accounts, every transaction and linked transfers.
- If a broker is chosen in the Viewing filter, you get that broker's trades instead.
- Some pages have their own report in this menu, for example **Transfers & links**, **Director's account**, **Reimbursements**, **Loans with people**, **Holdings by broker** and **Demat check**.

For tax reports, see [Reports and ITR](help:reports-and-itr).

### Alerts

The bell opens **Alerts & month close**. A number on the bell shows how many alerts there are; it turns red when something is urgent. See [Budgets, recurring and alerts](help:budgets-recurring-and-alerts).

### Light, dark or auto

The half-circle button changes the colours:

- **Light**: dark text on a light background.
- **Dark**: light text on a dark background.
- **Auto (follow Windows)**: follows the Windows setting, and changes when Windows changes.

Your choice is remembered by this browser.

### Lock and Quit

- The **lock** button locks the Hub at once. Your data is saved and encrypted, and you return to the log-in page.
- The **power** button quits Finance Hub completely, after asking you to confirm. Use it if the tray icon is not available.

See [Getting started](help:getting-started#opening-locking-and-quitting) for the difference between locking and quitting.

## Help and the user manual

The **?** button in the top bar opens the chapter of this manual that explains the page you are on.

You can also open **Settings › Help & user manual**. There you find:

- the list of chapters, with a short summary of each;
- **Search the manual…**: type a few words, such as "recovery key" or "UPI";
- **User manual (PDF)**: the whole manual as one PDF, to read or print;
- **Report a problem**: tell the Finance Hub team about something that doesn't work (see [Reporting a problem](help:reporting-a-problem));
- an **On this page** list on wide screens, to jump to a section of the chapter.

> **Good to know:** Administrators also see a **Design manual (internal)**. It describes how the Hub is built and is meant for the person who maintains it.
