Password Notes

Statement passwords are kept only inside your encrypted database — never in the program files.

When an import says a file needs a password, type it once and tick “save”; it is tried automatically next time.

Most banks use a mix of your name, date of birth and card / account digits — check the bank's e-mail that came with the statement.

After Updating

After updating a password, use Reset & Reload on the Add Statements page to re-parse the affected statements.
