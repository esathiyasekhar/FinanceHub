The Hub can mail a reminder the day before a payment is due (card bills, EMIs, loan repayments, regular payments, money borrowed from people) and a weekly report of everything due in the next two weeks, to the addresses you choose. When the Hub is locked

The mails for the coming two weeks are prepared whenever the Hub is unlocked and again whenever your data changes, so they can go out on time while the Hub is locked or nobody is using it.

{% if dpapi %}They are kept protected by your Windows login (they cannot be read on another computer or by another Windows user), so they are sent even after a restart.{% else %}On this computer they are kept only in memory, so they are sent while the Hub keeps running.{% endif %}

The Hub has to be running (it runs in the background — see the tray icon{% if not launcher %}; start it from the Finance Hub shortcut{% endif %}). A mail missed while the computer was off is sent when it starts, up to noon on the due date (weekly report: the same day). Mails reflect your data as of when they were prepared — import statements regularly.
